How to Play

LabControls
Paddle LabMouse: move over the canvas to steer the paddle. Keyboard: ↑/↓ or W/S. Keep the ball in play.
Break LabMouse: move to slide the paddle. Bounce the ball to clear all blocks and keep your lives.
Drop LabKeyboard: ←/→ move, ↓ soft drop, ↑ or Space rotate. Complete lines to score.
Risk PulseMouse: click Refresh Indicators to update the dashboard.
Cost EstimatorMouse/keyboard: choose a project type, enter square footage, set complexity with the slider, then click Calculate Range.

All labs use pure HTML5 + vanilla JS. LocalStorage where applicable. Ready for ARQS integration events.
